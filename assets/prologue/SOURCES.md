## beatrix.png — prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. This reference image is STYLE AND CHARACTER REFERENCE ONLY. Create a production sprite sheet on transparent background for the playable scientist Beatrix in a top-down three-quarter-view pixel-art game. Adult woman with medium brown skin, short voluminous curly brown hair, white open lab coat, teal blouse, charcoal trousers, dark ankle boots, compact cyan diagnostic tool in her right hand. Same identity/outfit in EVERY cell. Exactly 24 full-body individual sprites, uniform grid 8 columns by 3 rows. Canvas landscape 2048x1024. Equal cells, sprites centered horizontally, feet at same baseline relative to each cell, generous transparent gutters, no overlapping cells. Columns show in order facing east, southeast, south, southwest, west, northwest, north, northeast. Row 1 idle standing. Row 2 walking with left foot advanced. Row 3 walking with right foot advanced. Character size consistent, about 180 pixels tall within each cell. Entire character including hair and feet visible in all poses. Crisp pixel art with dark contours and detailed hand-shaded volumes matching the reference scientist. No floor, no shadow, no background, no text, no grid lines, no labels, no UI, no numbers, no other people, no weapons. Do not reproduce the screenshot or laboratory. Output only the transparent character sprite sheet.

O resultado possui 1774×887 pixels. As 24 regiões e âncoras dos pés foram medidas
diretamente na transparência, em vez de presumir o tamanho solicitado.

## laboratory.png — prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a transparent production environment sprite atlas for a top-down three-quarter-view pixel art science laboratory matching ONLY the style and equipment of the supplied reference. Output four completely separate objects in a regular 2-column by 2-row grid, canvas square 1536x1536, equal sized cells with at least 50px transparent gutters. Top-left: imposing cylindrical artificial-intelligence laboratory core tower, steel and bronze foundation, dark glass cylinder containing luminous CYAN suspended circuits, thick cables, three-quarter view, entire silhouette and base visible. Top-right: tall rectangular bioculture glass capsule with lush green fern ecosystem, teal water reservoir, gray metal top and base, cyan small lights, entire silhouette. Bottom-left: three dark steel server cabinets standing side by side as one unit, tiny teal indicator lights, entire silhouette. Bottom-right: wide low gray steel research console with two translucent cyan holographic screens, keyboard and narrow floor supports, complete silhouette. All objects have the SAME perspective, light from upper left, detailed shaded crisp pixel art dark outlines, metal scuffs, natural industrial materials and palette matching reference. Feet/base at the same relative baseline within each cell. Four standalone isolated props only, no connecting floor or wall, no shadows outside silhouettes, no text at all, no letters, NO Protocolo 17 inscription, no labels or logos, no characters, no red warning text, no UI, no grid lines, no background. Keep all objects well separated so each can be cropped and placed independently in the real game.

O resultado possui 1280×1280 pixels. As regiões respeitam a altura efetiva do núcleo
e das cápsulas; não se presume que a divisão vertical ocorra no meio da imagem.

## aurora_before.png — prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background illustration for the WORLD INTRODUCTION of a pixel art game, Aurora BEFORE its collapse. Primary request: a hopeful, functioning sustainable science-fiction city. Wide landscape composition 1536x1024. Three-quarter elevated view across clean teal water channels and terraced concrete bridges, thriving green wetland gardens, solar panels on buildings, elegant compact research towers with teal windows, small maintenance drones carrying supplies, a few tiny human silhouettes using pedestrian bridges. Nature and industrial infrastructure coexist in a carefully tended living city. Warm morning light from upper left, luminous cyan and green reflected in water, bronze accents, rich crisp hand-painted pixel-art textures and dark outlines consistent with a detailed top-down pixel game. Architecture and canals form a coherent city rather than a jumble of unrelated assets. Center should have calm lower-contrast open water and buildings so an opaque dialogue panel can be placed over it. No text, no labels, no logo, no numbers, no visible Protocolo 17 name, no red alarm, no abandoned or ruined equipment, no dramatic destruction, no large foreground character, no user interface. This is a reusable background image, not a screenshot.
