# Objetos do laboratório — revisão visual

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, usando a skill imagegen. Atlas original RGBA preservado em `laboratory_details.png`. A medição em `tools/measure_laboratory_details.py` apenas lê as silhuetas e escreve suas regiões; não altera os pixels. Seis peças: jardineira hidropônica, bancada botânica, carrinho, armário, terrário e bomba de recirculação.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent sprite atlas for a 2D science-fiction botanical laboratory game. Create ONE atlas with EXACTLY six isolated full object sprites, orderly 2 columns by 3 rows, with ample transparent gutters and no sprite overlap. Clean true transparent background. Render in rich detailed hand-painted pixel-art style with crisp pixels, polished metal, subtle wear, cyan illuminated edges, cool gray steel, warm brass fittings, lush emerald plants. Elevated three-quarter front camera for a top-down game, consistently viewing front and right side; front base nearly horizontal, mild depth, no full diamond isometric floor. All six sprites completely contained in their own cell. Layout: top left = broad futuristic hydroponic planter with lush ferns, broad-leaf plants and tiny white flowers, deep metal trough, irrigation tubes, thin cyan indicator strip, small water inlet; top right = botany research workbench with sample bottles, specimen leaf trays, microscope, small cyan monitor, drawers and cables; middle left = small medical laboratory utility cart on four wheels, tablet, folded tools, sample tubes; middle right = low wide archive storage cabinet, drawers, brass handles, glass sample compartments and cyan status lamps; bottom left = horizontal glass terrarium display full of luminous green plants with metal frame and cyan light strips; bottom right = compact water recycling pump with pipes, visible glass reservoir and water nozzle, cyan indicators, dark gray metal. Individual prop sprites only. No people, no floor tiles, no scene background, no text, no labels, no lettering, no drop shadow beyond a tiny contact shadow. Match a detailed pixel-art sci-fi botanical lab, not flat geometric icon art. Output portrait atlas approximately 1024x1536.
